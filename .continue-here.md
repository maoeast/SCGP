<decisions_made>

- Do not change the existing licensing/auth model.
  - Authorization remains based on top-level `moduleCode`.
- Do not elevate `fine-motor` or `soothing-aids` to top-level moduleCode.
  - They are equipment entry/catalog groups, not billing modules.
- Keep physical-equipment matching keyed by `meta_data.resourceCode`.
  - Avoid tying new assets to `legacy_id`.
- `/equipment` route authorization now resolves from the selected equipment entry back to the correct top-level module.
- Legacy links that only pass `query.module` are still accepted.
  - The new chain prefers `query.entry`.
- When a preselected equipment resource implies a different entry than the incoming query, Quick Entry auto-corrects to the resource's real entry.
- Development mock authorization was expanded in `src/stores/auth.ts` so the six equipment entries are all unlocked during local development without changing the top-level authorization model itself.
- Do not confuse equipment entry-group filtering with the quick-entry category-tab requirement.
  - entry-group filtering is now correct
  - the remaining category-tab requirement should use CSV-derived `metadata.sourceCategory`

</decisions_made>
